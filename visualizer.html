How to use the Visualizer
  • Search: Enter a Question ID in the top-left search bar to generate its similarity network.
  • Navigate: Click any outer node to re-root the graph and explore problems similar to it.
  • Open Details: Double-click the center (root) node, or click the title in the left panel, to open the problem details in a new tab.
  • Color Coding: Nodes are outlined based on difficulty (Green = Easy, Yellow = Medium, Red = Hard). The root node is always highlighted in blue. Premium problems are marked with a lock icon.
